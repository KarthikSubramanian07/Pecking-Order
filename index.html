Pecking Order
About Privacy GitHub

Rank anything.

Pecking Order is a fast, free tier list maker that runs entirely in your browser. Drop in pictures, drag them into tiers, and export a clean image of your opinions. No account, nothing uploaded.

How it works

  1. Add things. Drop in images or whole folders, paste links, type names, or search Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons and Openverse for pictures.
  2. Peck them into order. Drag each item into a tier from S to F, or rename the tiers to suit the argument.
  3. Export and settle it. Download a crisp PNG, copy it to the clipboard, or share it straight from your phone.

Features

  • Boards save to your device as you go. No sign-up, no server.
  • Your own images from files, folders, the clipboard or other websites.
  • Image search across Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons and Openverse, with credits kept.
  • Category packs: ready-made starter sets, contributed on GitHub.
  • Custom tier labels and colours, undo and redo, light and dark themes.
  • Image export in wide, portrait and story shapes.
  • Free and open source under the MIT licence.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Pecking Order is free and open source under the MIT licence. There are no ads, no premium tier and no sign-up.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts. Open the page and start ranking.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, in your browser's IndexedDB storage. Boards and images never leave your device unless you export or share them yourself. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes them.

Can I use my own images?

Yes. Drop in files or whole folders, paste from the clipboard, or drag pictures in from other web pages. They are stored on your device and never uploaded.

How do I add a category pack?

Packs are small JSON files in the GitHub repository: a title, a list of items, and where each item's picture lives on Wikipedia or Wikimedia Commons. Open a pull request that adds packs/your-pack/pack.json, or file a pack request.
